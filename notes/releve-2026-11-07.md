## IRS, Publication 15-A (2026) (https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15a), sick pay

- « Social security, Medicare, and FUTA taxes don't apply to sick pay paid more than 6 calendar months after the last calendar month in which the employee worked. »
- Exemple 1 : « Ralph's last day of work before becoming entitled to receive sick pay was December 5, 2025. … Sick pay paid to Ralph after June 30, 2026, isn't subject to social security, Medicare, or FUTA tax. »
- « Contributions to a sick pay plan made on behalf of employees with employees' pre-tax dollars under a cafeteria plan are employer contributions. »
- Contrat de groupe : « … figure the taxable sick pay by multiplying total sick pay by the percentage of the policy's cost that was contributed by the employer for the 3 policy years before the calendar year in which the sick pay is paid. »
- Exemple d'Alan : 2 000 $ par mois, l'employeur a payé 70 % du coût, « $1,400 ($2,000 × 70%) of each payment made to Alan is taxable sick pay. The remaining $600 … isn't taxable sick pay and isn't subject to employment taxes. »

## IRS, Publication 525 (https://www.irs.gov/publications/p525)

- « Cost paid by you. If you pay the entire cost of an accident or health plan, don't include any amounts you receive from the plan for personal injury or sickness as income on your tax return. »
- « Cafeteria plans. In most cases, if you're covered by an accident or health insurance plan through a cafeteria plan, and the amount of the insurance premiums wasn't included in your income, you aren't considered to have paid the premiums and you must include any benefits you receive in your income. »
- Contrats de dépendance : les versements forfaitaires (per diem) sont exclus jusqu'au plus grand de la limite par jour et des frais réels, diminué des remboursements ; calcul sur le Form 8853.

## IRS, Publication 502 (https://www.irs.gov/publications/p502)

- Les frais médicaux ne se déduisent que pour la part qui dépasse « 7.5% of your adjusted gross income (AGI) », et seulement en détaillant les déductions. Les primes de dépendance qualifiées comptent dans la limite par âge.

## IRS, Publication 915 (https://www.irs.gov/publications/p915), édition 2025 (dernière parue)

- « Base amount. Your base amount is: $25,000 if you are single, head of household, or qualifying surviving spouse; … $32,000 if you are married filing jointly ».
- Feuille de calcul, ligne 11 : « Enter $12,000 if married filing jointly; or $9,000 if single, head of household … », d'où les seuils de 34 000 $ et 44 000 $ ; 50 % puis 85 % au plus des prestations. L'exemple chiffré de la feuille (ligne 10 à 13 500 $, ligne 2 à 5 000 $, résultat 6 275 $) est repris en test.

## OPM, FEGLI Handbook (https://www.opm.gov/healthcare-insurance/life-insurance/reference-materials/feglihandbook.pdf)

- « Under Basic insurance, accidental dismemberment benefits for the loss of one hand, one foot, or eyesight in one eye are equal to one-half of your BIA. For the loss of two or more of these in the same accident, benefits are equal to your full BIA. Total AD&D benefits for a single accident, no matter how many losses occur, cannot be more than your full BIA. »
- Exemple d'Ileana : BIA de 42 000 $, 21 000 $ pour un bras, 21 000 $ de plus pour la vue des deux yeux, total plafonné à 42 000 $. Repris en test.

## Bureau of Labor Statistics, National Compensation Survey, mars 2026

- Communiqué « Employee Benefits in the United States, March 2026 », publié le 2026-09-25 (https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ebs2.nr0.htm).
- Les pages bls.gov refusent les scripts (403). Les séries NB ont donc été lues par l'API publique (requête POST sur api.bls.gov/publicAPI/v2/timeseries/data/, documentée sur https://www.bls.gov/developers/) le 2026-11-07. Fichier brut : `data-raw/bls-api-2026-11-07.json`, 373 séries. Fichier du site : `src/data/bls-ncs-2026.json`, produit par `scripts/data/build-bls.py`. Seules les valeurs de 2026 (période A01) sont retenues : 37 groupes, 84 taux d'accès, plus les dispositions des contrats pour 3 catégories d'employeurs.
- Droits : les publications du BLS sont dans le domaine public (https://www.bls.gov/opub/copyright-information.htm), avec citation de la source.
